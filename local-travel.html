Essential Local Travel Tips

Road Conditions

NH-7 & NH-107 are well maintained but narrow & winding. Monsoon landslides possible.

Best Travel Time

Early morning departures recommended. Avoid night travel in hills.

Altitude Sickness

Stay hydrated. Acclimatize at Joshimath / Guptkashi before high-altitude temples.

Network & Apps

Jio & Airtel work best. Download offline maps & IRCTC app.
