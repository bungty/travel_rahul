The Majestic Kedarnath Temple

Nestled in the Garhwal Himalayas, Kedarnath is one of the holiest shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva. Believed to have been built by the Pandavas, the temple stands at 3,583 meters and is only accessible by trek or helicopter.

“The darshan of Kedarnath Ji fills the heart with immense peace and devotion. It is not just a temple — it is a divine experience.” — Thousands of devotees every year
