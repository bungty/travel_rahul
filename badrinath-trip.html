The Sacred Abode of Lord Badrinath

Nestled in the Garhwal Himalayas on the banks of the Alaknanda River, Badrinath Temple is one of the most revered Vishnu temples in India. Established by Adi Shankaracharya, it marks the culmination of the holy Char Dham Yatra.
