Pro Tips for Outstation Yatris

Book Flights Early

Dehradun (DED) & Jolly Grant Airport get fully booked 3–4 months in advance during peak season.

Train Booking Strategy

Book Shatabdi / AC Express from Delhi to Haridwar. Tatkal opens 1 day before.

Luggage & Packing

Light woollens, raincoat, oxygen cylinder (if needed), and comfortable shoes.

Arrival Day Plan

Reach Haridwar / Rishikesh one day before your Char Dham departure for rest & acclimatization.

Recommended Arrival Airports / Stations
